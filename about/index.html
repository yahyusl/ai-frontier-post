About AI Frontier Post

AI Frontier Post is an independent publication covering AI news, important AI research, frontier lab developments, and AI product comparisons and reviews.

Who’s behind it

AI Frontier Post was founded by Yahya Laraki (Montreal, Canada). Contributing editors:

  • Priya Nair — Research Editor
  • Marcus Doyle — Senior Technology Writer
  • Sofia Almeida — Tutorials & Reviews Lead

What we cover

  • AI News — what’s happening in artificial intelligence, reported straight.
  • Research — important papers and ideas, explained in plain language.
  • Frontier Labs — what the leading AI labs are building and shipping.
  • Reviews — hands-on comparisons of AI products and tools.
  • Tutorials — practical guides for building and working with AI.

Editorial standards

  • Every factual claim is checked against primary sources — official lab blogs, papers, and documentation.
  • Analysis is labeled as analysis; reporting is labeled as reporting.
  • Sponsored content is always clearly labeled.
  • Affiliate links never affect our rankings or recommendations.

Corrections policy

Factual errors are corrected promptly. Significant corrections are noted on the article itself so readers can see what changed.

Affiliate disclosure

Some articles contain affiliate links. If you buy through them, the publication may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Sponsored posts are clearly labeled “Sponsored”.
