RUMOR: Qwen4 launching next week, per a claimed Alibaba Cloud remark at Apsara Amsterdam — Alibaba has only confirmed it is in training

A LocalLLaMA thread relaying an unnamed conference participant’s account claims Alibaba Cloud’s CTO said Qwen4 lands next week. Alibaba’s official word remains that the model is in training, with no date attached.

SASofia AlmeidaTutorials & Reviews Lead · October 29, 2026 · 5 min read
TL;DR: This is a rumor, not a launch announcement. A fast-moving LocalLLaMA thread claims Alibaba Cloud’s CTO told an Apsara Amsterdam participant that Qwen4 arrives next week. Nothing from Alibaba confirms that: the company’s official line is that Qwen4 is in training, with no date. Treat the date as unverified until Alibaba or Qwen says it themselves.
  • The claim (unverified): Qwen4 launches next week, per an unnamed participant’s account of a remark by Alibaba Cloud’s CTO at Apsara Amsterdam, relayed in a LocalLLaMA thread posted October 29, 2026.
  • The traction: the thread gathered roughly 1,100 upvotes and 219 comments within hours of posting, and no comment in the portion read debunked the headline claim.
  • The verified record: at its September 2026 Apsara Conference, Alibaba said Qwen4 is in training and outlined Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 at 5 to 10 trillion parameters — with no release date, sizes, or licensing terms.
  • The setting checks out, the quote does not: Alibaba Cloud did announce its first Netherlands cloud region on October 29 at an Apsara event in Amsterdam, but no Alibaba source found confirms a Qwen4 date.
  • Open weights unknown: thread commenters expect Flash and 27B variants to be open based on past Qwen releases, but that is speculation, and licensing terms are openly debated in the thread.

A Qwen4 launch date is racing around the local-model community this morning — and it rests on a single secondhand account. A thread on r/LocalLLaMA, posted early on October 29, 2026, says the Apsara conference is currently taking place in Amsterdam and that, according to a participant, Alibaba Cloud’s CTO said Qwen4 would launch next week. Within hours it had gathered roughly 1,100 upvotes and 219 comments, making it one of the fastest-moving Qwen threads of the week.

Read that again carefully: nobody named has confirmed anything on the record. The participant is unnamed, the remark is relayed rather than quoted, and Alibaba has said nothing about a Qwen4 date. This article reports what the community is saying, marks every unverified piece as unverified, and sets it against what Alibaba has actually put on the record — because the gap between those two things is the whole story.

What the thread actually claims#

The original post is short. Its entire factual content is the Amsterdam setting plus the relayed claim: a participant says the CTO said Qwen4 is coming next week. There is no video, no transcript, no named source, and no link to an Alibaba statement in the post itself. What followed was not a fact-check but a wishlist: commenters debated which Qwen4 variants would be open-weight, with several expecting Flash and a 27B model to be openly available based on how past Qwen generations shipped, and others worrying that licensing could tighten. None of that is confirmed either — it is community expectation, not company policy.

One thing the thread notably did not do is debunk itself. In the portion read, no comment challenged the headline claim that a launch remark was made; the argument was about what a launch would mean, not whether the remark happened. That is worth knowing, but it is not verification. A claim can travel fast and unchallenged for hours and still turn out to be a misunderstanding, a paraphrase stretched too far, or a date that was never meant as a commitment.

What Alibaba has actually said#

Alibaba’s own account of Qwen4 is far more restrained. At its annual Apsara Conference in Hangzhou on September 22, 2026, the company said its next-generation model, Qwen4, is currently in training, and laid out a roadmap in which the Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 series that follow are projected to reach 5 to 10 trillion parameters. Its published conference summary gives no release date, no model sizes, and no licensing terms for Qwen4. That is the verified record, and it has not visibly moved since.

The Amsterdam setting, at least, is real. On October 29, Alibaba Cloud announced its first public cloud region in the Netherlands, with the announcement datelined Amsterdam and tied to the 2026 Apsara Conference. So a Qwen conversation happening in Amsterdam this week is plausible — but a conference taking place is not evidence that a particular sentence was spoken there, and Alibaba’s Amsterdam announcements found so far concern cloud infrastructure, not a Qwen4 date.

There is also a recent cautionary tale in the same forums. Earlier this week, a separate Qwen4 thread built a 1M-context claim on a PR screenshot, and its poster later walked the date claim back after being challenged. That history does not disprove today’s claim, but it is exactly why a secondhand date deserves a rumor label rather than a headline that treats it as fact.

Why the community is primed to believe it#

Qwen is the rare frontier family that local builders can actually run. Alibaba has made a point of its open-weight 27B line’s popularity with developers, and each recent generation has shipped a capable smaller model alongside its API-only flagships. That track record is why a “next week” whisper lands so hard: for self-hosters, a new Qwen generation is not an abstract benchmark story but a download they can plan hardware around, and the thread is full of exactly that planning — VRAM budgets, creative-writing hopes, and worries about whether the new generation keeps the permissive licensing that made the last one useful.

The licensing worry is the most substantive thread in the discussion, and it cuts against blind enthusiasm. Commenters raised Qwen’s custom license terms and an internal-use exception, and debated an attribution trigger tied to very large monthly usage. Those details are unverified in the thread and Alibaba has published no Qwen4 license to check them against — which is itself a reason to wait: the license may matter more to local users than the launch date.

What to watch#

Three things would turn this rumor into news. First, an on-the-record statement from Alibaba Cloud or the Qwen team confirming a date — a post, a keynote clip, or a press release, not another relayed account. Second, a model card or weights listing, which is how past Qwen open releases have actually materialized. Third, license text: whatever Qwen4’s terms turn out to be will decide whether the local community’s excitement survives contact with reality.

Until one of those appears, the honest summary is short. The community believes Qwen4 may be days away, on the strength of one unnamed participant’s account. Alibaba has confirmed only that Qwen4 is in training. If next week comes and goes without an announcement, the rumor will have cost nothing but a few days of anticipation; if it lands, the thread will have called it first. Either way, the date itself remains unverified, and this article will be updated when — or if — Alibaba says otherwise.
