Anthropic and Hg launch a European AI consultancy: Claude engineers embedded in client firms, hubs in London and Berlin

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · October 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Its consultants and engineers will work inside European businesses to build new AI products and integrate AI into operations — a direct challenge to the traditional consulting giants.

Anthropic has agreed with London private-equity firm Hg to launch an independent AI consultancy in Europe by the end of the year, with hubs in London and Berlin, the Financial Times reported on October 23. It would be the first standalone consultancy the frontier lab has backed in Europe — and a direct assault on the territory of Accenture, Deloitte and the Big Four.

Engineers inside the building

Under the agreement, consultants and engineers from the new company will be embedded inside European businesses to help them design new AI-powered products or support projects already under way, PYMNTS reported, citing the FT's account. The firm's client work will also extend across Hg's own portfolio of companies. The target market is business-software firms and other white-collar industries pursuing AI adoption, according to Dealroom's summary of the report.

On the money side, Anthropic will fund its share of the venture from its own balance sheet, while Hg invests through the funds it manages, including the London-listed HgCapital Trust, TipRanks reported. One caveat: the story rests entirely on the FT's unnamed sources. Both Anthropic and Hg declined to comment to the FT, and neither immediately replied to PYMNTS' request for comment.

The Ode playbook, now in Europe

The European venture is a regional copy of a template Anthropic already validated in the United States. In July 2026, the lab co-founded Ode, a standalone services company with Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman — a planned $1.5 billion investment combining Anthropic's models with experienced AI engineers and operators, with Goldman Sachs, Apollo Global and General Atlantic among the other backers. OpenAI has run the same play since May 2026 with its Deployment Company, built alongside TPG, Advent, Bain Capital and Brookfield.

The Anthropic–Hg relationship is not new: the two have worked together since 2024, and in September Hg extended its existing collaboration with Anthropic to bring agentic AI capabilities to Hg's portfolio companies, PYMNTS reported. Hg already employs around 150 AI specialists who deploy AI across its holdings; one source told the FT the new consultancy would operate alongside that team rather than replace it. Hg's portfolio becomes a ready-made client pipeline — its holdings include the accountancy group Azets and the internet-of-things provider AMDT.

The Big Four just got a new rival

The FT's analysis, echoed by Aju Press, is blunt: Anthropic's direct entry into consulting could threaten Accenture, Deloitte, EY, KPMG and PwC — the firms that have been earning lucrative fees steering companies through technology shifts, and in some cases reselling Claude itself to large clients. A lab that sends its own engineers into the client's office is now chasing the same budgets.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Anthropic–Hg consultancy?

It is an independent European AI consultancy backed by Anthropic and London private-equity firm Hg, expected to launch by the end of 2026 with hubs in London and Berlin. Its consultants and engineers will work inside European businesses to build new AI products and services and integrate AI into current operations, and it will also serve companies in Hg's portfolio.

Who is funding the new consultancy?

Anthropic will fund its share of the venture from its own balance sheet, while Hg invests through the funds it manages, including the London-listed HgCapital Trust. The reporting did not disclose an investment figure.

Is this Anthropic's first services business?

It is the first standalone consultancy Anthropic has backed in Europe. In the United States, Anthropic co-founded Ode in July 2026 with Blackstone and Hellman & Friedman, a standalone services firm with a planned $1.5 billion investment. OpenAI has used a similar playbook since May 2026 with its Deployment Company, built with TPG, Advent, Bain Capital and Brookfield.

Did Anthropic or Hg confirm the deal?

No. The Financial Times' October 23 report cites people familiar with the matter. Both companies declined to comment to the FT, and neither immediately replied to PYMNTS' request for comment.
