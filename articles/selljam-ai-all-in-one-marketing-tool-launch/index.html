Selljam AI goes live: one workspace for research, writing, images and publishing — from $9 a month

Selljam AI is live. The Porto-based tool, announced on November 1, folds research, writing, image creation and social publishing into a single workspace for small businesses, online sellers and content creators — organised around a Brand Guide it drafts from the business's own website.

Selljam AI, an all-in-one AI marketing tool for small businesses, online sellers and content creators, is now live, according to a launch announcement dated November 1, 2026. It brings research, writing, visuals and publishing into one workspace, using a shared Brand Guide to keep content consistent across formats — aimed at owners who handle their own marketing and currently juggle a chatbot, a design app and a scheduler.

Founder James Mew said he built the tool after struggling with consistency himself. “Consistency is something I struggled with. I knew content, social media and posting frequently was needed to grow the business. But I was ‘too busy managing the business’ to really give it the attention it deserved,” Mew said in the announcement. “I wanted to find a way to make content creation and social media marketing simple, affordable, and easy to use. So I built Selljam AI to help people like me grow their brand or business.” The announcement says Mew has built and grown online businesses for more than 22 years, led ecommerce across 14 online shops in Europe and South Africa, and reached more than 45,000 learners with a LinkedIn Learning course on Google Gemini. Those background claims are the company's own and have not been independently verified.

A Brand Guide built from the business's website#

Setup starts with a website address. Selljam AI scans the site and drafts a Brand Guide covering the business's offers, visual style and tone of voice, and the guide then informs each post, article and email, so owners can reuse their business details across workflows. On its own site, the company says the guide is ready in 30 to 60 seconds and covers basics, visuals, offers, voice and marketing — including differentiators, benefits and proof.

Research, images and publishing in one place#

The workspace covers three jobs. Research and writing includes competitor and source research, keyword discovery, an AI social media post generator, and tools for writing articles, emails and offer pages. Images and design includes an AI product image generator, a photo studio and Studio templates that use the brand's visual style. Review and publishing adds content checks, scheduling and publishing, plus a Library for saving and reusing content.

Publishing spans 11 platforms, with supported formats depending on the channel: X, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Bluesky, Threads, Google Business Profile and WordPress. The four working modes range from Editor, for writing freely, to Step-by-Step, Guide Me and Done for Me, for increasing AI assistance. The company's site also describes a Brief, Draft, Score and Publish flow, in which drafts can optionally be scored on intent, structure, authority, clarity and visibility before publishing.

Pricing starts at $9, with credits doing the counting#

Monthly plans start at $9. The company's pricing page lists Starter at $9 a month for 1,000 credits, Pro at $29 for 3,300 credits and Max at $79 for 9,300 credits. Pay-as-you-go credit packs start at $5, at $0.01 per credit — $5 for 500, $15 for 1,500 or $30 for 3,000. Monthly plans include a seven-day trial with 300 credits, and a payment card is required; the company's FAQ says the plan price is charged after seven days unless the trial is cancelled. All plans include unlimited brands and social profiles, plus access to the AI Creators Club's courses, resources, templates and automations, according to the announcement.

Credits are charged when actions are completed. The company's FAQ puts a social post with a high-quality image at 45 credits, a 1,000-word article with a high-quality image at 60 credits and a fast-quality image at 15 credits, and says content and brand information are not used to train models. What the launch does not yet show is independent testing: no review or hands-on evaluation of the writing quality, image output or publishing reliability was available at launch, and the announcement was distributed through PRUnderground, whose syndication pages carry a third-party-content disclaimer. The $9 entry price is low, but the credit arithmetic — not the headline price — is what will decide whether it stays cheap for a business publishing every day.

Sources: Selljam AI launch announcement via PRUnderground, November 1, 2026 (primary source); Selljam AI, company homepage and pricing/FAQ pages — checked November 1, 2026. Founder background and product claims are company statements from the announcement and the company's site.
