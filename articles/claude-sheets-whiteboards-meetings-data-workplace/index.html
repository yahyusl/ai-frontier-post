Anthropic launches Claude Sheets, Whiteboards and Meetings — Claude moves into the office

Anthropic announced Claude Sheets, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Meetings about an hour ago at a San Francisco event, pushing Claude out of the developer’s terminal and into the rest of the office — with Claude Data, its dashboards product, going generally available the same day.

What we checked: Anthropic’s own announcement, published November 10, 2026, cross-checked against TechCrunch’s same-day report (published 12:30 p.m. ET).

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · November 10, 2026 · 5 min read
TL;DR: Anthropic launched Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards in beta and made Claude Data generally available on November 10, 2026, with Claude Meetings coming soon. Sheets, Whiteboards and Data are shared artifacts a team builds with Claude — spreadsheets, boards and dashboards that carry the same context from one to the next — on paid plans across web, desktop and mobile.
  • What: Claude Sheets, Claude Whiteboards and Claude Meetings, plus Claude Data going generally available.
  • When: announced November 10, 2026 at a San Francisco event; TechCrunch published its report at 12:30 p.m. ET.
  • Availability: Sheets and Whiteboards in beta on paid plans, on web, desktop and mobile. Data generally available. Meetings coming soon.
  • Connectors: Claude Marketplace lists more than 9,000 connectors and plugins; interactive connectors embed Adobe, Atlassian, Salesforce (beta), NetSuite and Workday (beta) inside Claude.
  • Enterprise controls: admins decide who can use which products, set spend limits, and get audit logs plus a Compliance API covering more than 100 security tools; each cloud session runs in its own isolated virtual machine.
  • Proof point: Anthropic’s legal team used Claude to build a Slack-based review tool that cut marketing reviews from three days to 24 hours.

Here is the pitch in one workflow. A team opens a Whiteboard in the middle of a meeting and talks through a plan with Claude, which turns the discussion into an organized visual workspace — timelines, deadlines, action items. The same context carries straight into Sheets, where the team and Claude build the budget tracker together: formulas, tabs, dropdowns, no re-explaining the plan. Then Data takes the numbers and turns them into a dashboard, a written data story, or a narrated short video. One thread of context, three artifacts, no copy-paste between apps.

How the pieces fit together#

Anthropic’s own demo makes the flow concrete: a team planning a Huckleberry Pie launch for a bakery called GeeGolly Good Pies. On the Whiteboard they lay out an illustrated launch timeline with Claude; in Sheets they build the tracker that prices it out; in Data they watch the numbers land. The point of the demo is not the pie — it is that Claude carries the same context through every step, so an idea can move from board to spreadsheet to dashboard without anyone restating the details.

Under the hood, the context comes from connectors. Claude reaches the tools a team already uses through the Model Context Protocol, the open standard Anthropic introduced for connecting AI to apps, and its Marketplace now lists more than 9,000 connectors and plugins. Interactive connectors go a step further: partners can embed their apps directly inside Claude, so you work in Adobe or Atlassian without switching tabs, and plugins from Salesforce, NetSuite and Workday come with built-in skills — a seller prepping for a call, an accountant analyzing financial data, an HR team handling time off and expenses.

What launched today#

The announcement, made at a San Francisco event on November 10, covers four products at once. Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards are new artifact types, both in beta on paid plans across web, desktop and mobile. Claude Data — formerly Claude Dashboards, which Anthropic introduced in beta on October 8 — is now generally available, though its certified queries and live sharing features stay in beta. Claude Meetings, which adds Claude to a video call as a visible participant, is coming soon according to Anthropic; TechCrunch reports its beta is for Team and Enterprise plans and works with Google Meet at launch.

Access is tiered. On Team plans all three shipped products are on by default; on Enterprise plans Whiteboards and Sheets start off by default, while Data is scheduled to turn on by default on November 19, 2026. That is the enterprise rollout in miniature: on for the teams that asked, gated for the ones with compliance reviews to run first.

The enterprise plumbing#

Anthropic is selling control as hard as capability. On Enterprise plans, admins decide who can use which products and connectors and set spend limits at a granular level. Claude auto-connect, now in beta on eligible Team and Enterprise plans, makes connecting an app like Notion, Atlassian or Dropbox a one-time setup, after which employees are connected the first time they use it — and admins can limit what each connector does, so Claude might be able to read Slack but not send messages on a team’s behalf.

The audit trail is built in: security teams get audit logs and a Compliance API that works with more than 100 security and compliance tools, including Splunk and CrowdStrike, and data from Enterprise organizations is encrypted in transit and at rest. In the new Claude experience on Enterprise, each cloud session runs in its own isolated virtual machine, with connector credentials kept outside it.

Why now — and who this is really for#

The pattern is borrowed from Claude Code, which changed how Anthropic’s engineers work by letting them describe ideas and review what Claude built instead of coding line by line. Anthropic’s argument is that the same shift is now available to teams that never write code. Its legal team built a Slack-based review tool with Claude that draws redlines from the team’s own playbook, cutting marketing reviews from three days to 24 hours; its finance team turned a long executive business update into an interactive page the whole team updates together.

That framing is also the competitive tell. The launch puts Anthropic directly against Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, the suites that already own the meeting, the spreadsheet and the whiteboard — and it lands a day after Anthropic’s own Claude Fable 5.2 launch and the same day Google made Gemini 4 Carbon generally available. The race is no longer just about the smartest model; it is about whose assistant lives where the work already happens. Anthropic is betting the answer is the artifact that follows you from the meeting to the spreadsheet to the dashboard — and that the Model Context Protocol underneath it is the moat.

What to watch: whether Meetings ships on the promised “soon” and how enterprises react to an AI sitting in live calls, and whether the 9,000-connector Marketplace turns into real workflow lock-in or just a long list. The Sheets and Whiteboards betas are the test — if teams actually plan and budget inside Claude, the office suite just got a fourth contender.

AFP take: This is Anthropic’s clearest move yet from chatbot to workplace platform — and the strongest idea in it is not any single tool but the shared context that carries a plan from whiteboard to spreadsheet to dashboard. The risk is equally clear: a beta spreadsheet and a coming-soon meeting bot are promises, not products, and the enterprise controls are doing a lot of heavy lifting for trust. If Meetings lands fast and works in real calls, Google and Microsoft have a genuine problem; if it slips, this is a nice demo of artifacts that were already in Claude.

Frequently asked questions

What are Claude Sheets and Whiteboards?

New collaborative artifact types in Claude, launched in beta on November 10, 2026 for paid plans on web, desktop and mobile. Sheets lets a team build and edit spreadsheets together with Claude — formulas, multiple tabs and dropdowns — and files export to Excel. Whiteboards is a shared real-time board where a team lays out a plan with Claude, then turns it into timelines, action items, documents or slide decks.

What is Claude Data, and how is it different from Claude Dashboards?

Claude Data is the new name for Claude Dashboards, which Anthropic introduced in beta on October 8, 2026. It connects Claude to company data sources such as Snowflake and Salesforce and builds dashboards, visual data stories and narrated short videos. It became generally available on November 10, 2026, though certified queries and live sharing remain in beta.

When can teams use Claude Meetings?

Not yet. Anthropic says Claude Meetings is coming soon and will let teams add Claude to a call to take notes and update the documents they are working on during the conversation. TechCrunch reports the beta is for Team and Enterprise plans and works with Google Meet at launch, with Claude joining as a visible participant and starting work while the call is still going.
