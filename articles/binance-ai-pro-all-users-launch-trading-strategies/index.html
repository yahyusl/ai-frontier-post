Binance AI Pro goes public: an AI agent that turns trading ideas into tested strategies — with a free tier

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · October 19, 2026 · 5 min read
TL;DR: Binance announced AI Pro on October 19, 2026 and opened it to all users globally: a conversational AI agent that turns plain-language trading ideas into visual, testable strategies. The free Standard tier gets 250,000 monthly credits with DeepSeek access; Premium costs 19.99 USDC/month for 10 million credits and frontier models including ChatGPT Sol, ChatGPT Astra, and Claude Opus. Live trading needs explicit user approval and runs in isolated sub-accounts — up to 10 strategies per user.
  • Who: Binance, announcing on its official blog on October 19, 2026.
  • What: Binance AI Pro — a finance-focused AI agent that converts plain-language trading ideas into automated strategies, part of the Binance Intelligence suite (with Binance Agent OS and Binance AI).
  • Rollout: from 2026-10-19 to all users globally on the website and the Binance app; a paid beta that began March 25, 2026 keeps running alongside it.
  • Pricing: Standard tier is free — 250,000 monthly credits with DeepSeek access; Premium is 19.99 USDC/month — 10 million credits plus ChatGPT Sol, ChatGPT Astra, and Claude Opus where available, with credit top-ups.
  • Guardrails: 10 strategies max per user across paper trading, live, and signals; live deployment needs explicit approval, runs in manually funded isolated sub-accounts, and can be paused or revoked. Spot and USDS-M Futures supported, subject to regional restrictions. No historical backtesting at launch.
  • The quote: “Binance AI Pro is about access: professional-style strategy building, no coding required, with the user in control at every step,” said Jeff Li, Binance’s VP of Product.

From chat prompt to live strategy

Binance announced AI Pro on October 19, and rolled it out to all users globally on the website and the Binance app from that day. It is a finance-focused AI agent that takes a trading idea described in plain language — the assets, the risk limits, the market conditions to watch — and turns it into an automated strategy rendered as an editable visual workflow. No coding, no API bot to configure, no separate app to download.

On desktop, the strategy appears on a Strategy Canvas beside the chat: click any step to ask questions, change parameters, add or remove elements. On the Binance app, strategies are built and refined entirely through chat. Binance gives example prompts like a short daily market report, a dollar-cost averaging plan for Bitcoin, and a conservative strategy for a $1,000 account. Under the hood, the agent combines Binance's market data and research with selected third-party sources, and supports market monitoring, daily reports, alerts, and signal generation.

Paper trade first, then go live — carefully

Every strategy can be tested with paper trading — hypothetical funds — before a cent of real money is involved. Going live is gated: it requires explicit user approval, the strategy runs in an isolated sub-account that the user funds manually, and it can be paused, adjusted, or revoked at any time. Revising a strategy requires a fresh approval before it trades again. Binance caps each user at 10 strategies total across simulated, live, and signal-only modes.

The tool supports Spot and USDS-M Futures strategies, subject to regional restrictions. One notable absence at launch: historical backtesting is not available — Binance offers automated code testing and paper trading instead. The company also flags the obvious: AI outputs are not guaranteed, and trading can lose money.

The pricing: a free tier, and frontier models for pay

The launch adds a free Standard tier: 250,000 monthly credits and access to DeepSeek. Premium costs 19.99 USDC per month and brings 10 million monthly credits, the option to top up, and access to frontier models where available — ChatGPT Sol, ChatGPT Astra, and Claude Opus on top of DeepSeek. That model menu is the headline for anyone watching the AI pricing wars: a crypto exchange is now reselling access to frontier models as part of a trading product.

The wider rollout follows a paid beta that began March 25, 2026 at 07:00 UTC for a limited group. Existing beta subscriptions stay active, the beta and the new product can run side by side, and chat history and strategies can optionally be migrated. Binance previewed the broader release during its Binance Intelligence livestream on October 5 — AI Pro sits inside that suite, alongside Binance Agent OS and the Binance AI assistant.

Why this launch matters

Algorithmic trading was never about access to capital — it was about access to tooling. Binance is betting that a conversational agent removes the last technical barrier: describing a strategy in words and letting the model write the code, test it, and run it inside the exchange's own guardrails. The question is whether retail strategies built this way survive contact with real markets — paper trading validates the mechanics, not the edge. But the direction is clear: the exchange that owns the order flow now wants to own the strategy layer too.

“Binance AI Pro is about access: professional-style strategy building, no coding required, with the user in control at every step,” said Jeff Li, Binance's VP of Product. The free tier makes it easy to try; the safeguards — isolated accounts, explicit approvals, revocability — make it survivable to try. Whether it produces alpha is a question no announcement can answer.
