ChatGPT Enterprise is now on AWS Marketplace — buy it through your AWS bill

OpenAI put ChatGPT Enterprise on AWS Marketplace today, November 9, 2026, letting companies buy the four-product bundle — Chat, ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots — through their existing AWS account and count the spend toward the AWS commitments they already hold.

What we checked: OpenAI’s announcement, “ChatGPT Enterprise now available in AWS Marketplace” (November 9, 2026), and Unite.AI’s report of the launch.

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · November 9, 2026 · 5 min read
TL;DR: OpenAI launched ChatGPT Enterprise on AWS Marketplace on November 9, 2026. Companies can now buy the Chat, ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots bundle through their AWS account, billed like any other AWS purchase, and draw down existing Enterprise Discount Program or private-pricing commitments. There is no public price — deals are private offers.
  • Who: OpenAI — ChatGPT Enterprise, sold through AWS Marketplace.
  • When: Announced and available November 9, 2026.
  • Bundle: Chat, ChatGPT Work, Codex and dots in one bundle.
  • Billing: Billed through the customer’s existing AWS account; Enterprise Discount Program, private pricing and Private Pricing Addendum commitments can be applied.
  • Scale: More than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week, according to OpenAI.
  • Also available: ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services for eligible customers; the listing complements OpenAI models in Amazon Bedrock.

“Time that would otherwise go to procurement can go toward using AI to deliver more value for our customers.”

That is John Peterson, chief technology officer at Sophos, describing what changed when ChatGPT Enterprise showed up on AWS Marketplace. For his team, the news is not a new model or a new feature. It is a shorter road from deciding to buy to actually using the thing — one channel, one bill, and an existing pile of AWS commitments to draw it down against.

Peterson’s line is the whole story of today’s announcement. OpenAI already has the users. What it wants now is the enterprise buying channel, and AWS Marketplace is the shortest route to the companies whose cloud budgets are already committed.

What happened#

OpenAI announced on November 9, 2026 that ChatGPT Enterprise is available through AWS Marketplace. The listing gives organizations a single place to discover, procure and deploy it, billed through the AWS account they already have. Customers with an Enterprise Discount Program agreement, private pricing or a Private Pricing Addendum can apply those existing commitments toward ChatGPT Enterprise.

The bundle is four products in one. Chat is the familiar conversational surface. ChatGPT Work takes on multi-step research, analysis and projects, using connected apps and files to produce documents, presentations, spreadsheets and reports. Codex helps developers understand codebases, build features, fix bugs, run tests and review changes — see how it stacks up against rival coding agents — see our hands-on with Codex CLI. Dots are always-on agents that track updates, keep documents and analyses current, and surface the results and decisions that need attention.

Eligible customers can also access ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Financial Services through the marketplace, with eligibility confirmed through an OpenAI or AWS account team. OpenAI published no price and no per-seat rate; the listing is priced through private offers, and AWS handles the purchasing and billing while OpenAI manages the product experience.

Why it matters#

The number OpenAI leads with is not a price but a headcount: more than 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week. The enterprise version is the same familiar product, sold where the buyers already spend. For a company with a big AWS commitment to burn down, ChatGPT Enterprise stops being a new line item and becomes a use for money already promised.

It also draws a clean line between the two ways OpenAI sells into AWS shops. Developers build AI applications and agents with OpenAI models in Amazon Bedrock; teams across the business use ChatGPT Enterprise in their daily work. OpenAI positions the two as complementary, and says customers get the same models, features and functionality through the marketplace as they do buying direct, with new features released at the same time. The marketplace launch follows an April 28, 2026 announcement in which OpenAI and AWS introduced limited previews of OpenAI models on AWS, Codex on AWS and Amazon Bedrock Managed Agents — today’s move adds the procurement channel for the finished bundle. For the bigger picture on what enterprises are actually spending, see our enterprise AI spending roundup.

The catch is what is missing: no public price, no named regions, and no word on whether the listing will appear on other clouds’ marketplaces. The AWS listing also states that buying through the marketplace does not guarantee all customer data stays on AWS infrastructure — the deployment label is not a data-location guarantee. And if you want to put ChatGPT to work on your own stack first, our guide to connecting a custom MCP server shows how to give it your own tools.

What’s next#

For now the move is aimed squarely at AWS-centric enterprises: find the listing, buy through the existing account, and let the spend count toward commitments. Eligible customers in healthcare and financial services can ask their OpenAI or AWS account team about those versions. The thing to watch is whether OpenAI repeats the play on Microsoft’s and Google’s marketplaces — today’s announcement says nothing about either — and whether the EDP drawdown actually unblocks the deals that procurement had been holding up.

Frequently asked questions

How do I buy ChatGPT Enterprise on AWS Marketplace?

Find the listing in AWS Marketplace and buy through your existing AWS account — AWS handles purchasing and billing, while OpenAI manages the product experience. Contact your OpenAI or AWS account team to confirm eligibility and get started.

What is included in the ChatGPT Enterprise bundle on AWS?

Four products in one bundle: Chat, for conversations; ChatGPT Work, for delegated multi-step research and projects; Codex, for coding help; and dots, always-on agents that track updates and surface decisions needing attention.

Does buying through AWS Marketplace change the price or the product?

No — OpenAI says AWS customers get the same models, features and functionality as buying direct, with new features released at the same time. There is no public price; pricing is through private offers. Eligible spend can count toward existing AWS Enterprise Discount Program or Private Pricing Addendum commitments.
