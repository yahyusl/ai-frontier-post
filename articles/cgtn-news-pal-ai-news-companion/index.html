CGTN launches News Pal, an AI news companion that summarizes coverage of China and the world

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · October 22, 2026 · 4 min read
TL;DR: On October 22, 2026, Chinese state broadcaster CGTN launched News Pal, a web-based AI news companion that aggregates and summarizes reporting about China and the world into concise briefings. Its chat feature answers follow-up questions grounded in the reporting, with links to the original articles for verification.
  • Who: CGTN, China’s state broadcaster.
  • What: News Pal, a web-based AI-powered news companion that aggregates and summarizes coverage about China and the world.
  • When: announced Thursday, October 22, 2026 (press release published 2:02 a.m. ET).
  • Summarization: draws on multiple reports — sometimes dozens — on the same topic, distilling key points and cutting repetition; shows how a story is covered from different perspectives.
  • Chat: answers follow-up questions by searching available articles and grounding answers in that reporting rather than a language model’s internal knowledge; links to the original reports for verification.
  • Context: CGTN already uses AI for script translation, audio news generation, personalized recommendations and daily briefings; News Pal is framed as the next step.

China's state broadcaster CGTN has launched News Pal, an AI-powered news companion that aggregates and summarizes reporting about China and the world into concise briefings. The web-based service went live this month, the network announced on Thursday. (PR Newswire)

The pitch is focus rather than novelty: a single place that turns real-time reporting from a range of outlets into a digest you can actually finish. Instead of drowning in dozens of tabs, readers get the key points with the repetition removed.

Dozens of reports, one digest

News Pal's summarization feature draws on multiple reports — sometimes dozens — on the same topic, distilling the key points and cutting out repetition to produce a clear digest. That multi-source approach also lets users see how the same developing story is covered from different perspectives, which CGTN positions as a way to get up to speed quickly without reading everything.

A chat that answers from the reporting

The second half of the product is a chat feature for follow-up questions. When you ask something, the system searches available articles, identifies the relevant reports, and grounds its answers in that content — rather than relying solely on a large language model's internal knowledge. Answers include links to the original reports so readers can verify the details themselves. (Morningstar / PR Newswire)

That design — retrieval over generation — is the interesting technical choice. It treats the model's memory as a liability: every answer traces back to reporting you can click through to.

State media's AI playbook

News Pal is the latest in a string of AI moves at CGTN. The network says it has been expanding its use of AI across its news services, from script translation and audio news generation to personalized recommendations and daily briefings. News Pal, the network said, "marks a new step in its use of AI to help audiences worldwide become better informed more easily."

It also marks a shift in who builds the AI news interface. For years, aggregation and AI summaries were Silicon Valley's product to sell back to publishers; here a state broadcaster is building its own — with a China-focused corpus and answers grounded in the reporting it draws on.
