OpenAI admits a GPT-6.2 billing error: your limits will now run out up to 40% faster

OpenAI undercounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage at launch, so paying subscribers got more than they paid for — until now. The fix means the same work eats 40% more of your Astra allowance and 25% more of Sol’s, with one banked reset by way of apology.

PNPriya NairResearch Editor · November 26, 2026 · 6 min read
TL;DR: OpenAI has admitted it undercounted usage of GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol since their November 17 launch. A fix now in effect means the same work consumes more of your plan’s allowance: you will hit your limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than last week. OpenAI will not charge for the difference, other models are unaffected, and every Go, Plus, Pro and Business account gets one banked usage reset.
  • The error: a billing mistake at the GPT-6.2 launch incorrectly discounted Astra and Sol usage for most users.
  • The effect: limits are now reached 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra and 25% faster on Sol, compared with last week.
  • Who gets the reset: one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro and Business, redeemable from November 26.
  • What doesn’t change: subscription prices, other models, and no retroactive charges for past usage.
  • Next change: from December, Plus, Pro and Business share one token-based allowance across ChatGPT and Codex.

If your ChatGPT plan felt more generous this past week, it was — by accident. OpenAI has admitted that a billing error at the launch of GPT-6.2 meant most users’ Astra and Sol usage was counted at a discount, so the same prompts and coding sessions stretched further than they were supposed to. The company has now fixed the counting, and the correction bites immediately: the same work will use more of your plan’s allowance, and you will reach your limits 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra and 25% faster on Sol than you did last week.

The admission came from Thibault “Tibo” Sottiaux, OpenAI’s head of Product & Platform, who oversees ChatGPT, Codex and the API platform, in a post on X on November 26. “We made a billing mistake at launch of GPT-6.2 that incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most users,” he wrote. “We’ve fixed this now, so the same work will use more of your plan’s usage allowance.”

What OpenAI got wrong#

Astra and Sol arrived on November 17, with Astra positioned as OpenAI’s most capable model for demanding professional work and advanced coding, and Sol as the faster, more efficient option for everyday tasks. They have been available in ChatGPT Work and Codex since launch, and a version of Sol has powered the regular Chat tab for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users since November 23. From day one, though, the metering was wrong: usage was discounted, so subscribers burned allowance more slowly than OpenAI intended. The company has not said how the error happened or how many users were affected, only that it applied to “most users” of the two models. Other models were not affected.

What changes for you#

The headline numbers are the ones Sottiaux gave: compared with last week, limits arrive 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol. Two things soften the blow, at least on paper. First, OpenAI says both models should still stretch further than their predecessors — Astra beyond GPT-6 Astra, Sol beyond GPT-6.1 Sol — because the new models are cheaper to run. Second, nothing about pricing changes: this is a correction to how usage counts against existing limits, not a price rise, and OpenAI says it will not charge anyone for the extra usage already consumed. The timing is awkward in another way: from December, Plus, Pro and Business plans will share a single allowance across ChatGPT and Codex, measured in tokens, as the Work agent’s capabilities move into the main ChatGPT experience — so the month’s budgeting gets simpler just as the allowance itself gets tighter.

The banked reset, and how to use it#

By way of apology, OpenAI is adding one banked reset to every account on Go, Plus, Pro and Business, redeemable from November 26. A banked reset is a one-time refresh of your allowance that sits saved on your account until you choose to spend it. You redeem it in Usage settings on the web, or under Settings and Usage & billing in the desktop app — the mobile app does not offer the option. It only works once at least one of the allowances it covers has less than 80% remaining. On Plus it restores the five-hour and weekly allowances to 100%; on Go it restores the monthly allowance. It is worth holding until you actually need it: once spent, it is gone, and the new counting stays.

The reaction#

The announcement landed badly. On X, the post drew more than 11,000 likes and 4,100 replies within hours, according to Meridian’s daily brief, and the tone was overwhelmingly negative: the commentator NIK called it a “total rugpull and betrayal of users,” another user posted a meme of Claude and Gemini watching OpenAI shoot itself in the face, and a third said the move was pushing users to Gemini and Claude. On Reddit, the same frustration showed up as soon as users connected the announcement to the limits they had been hitting: a thread titled “you will reach your limits 40% faster using GPT-6.2” gathered 204 upvotes and 76 comments on r/ChatGPT, and another on r/OpenAI asked whether the company was “snacking” 40% of Astra usage. The underlying complaint is less about the arithmetic than about trust: subscribers had built workflows around the allowance they were actually getting, and the correction arrived with a day’s notice and a single reset as compensation.

What to watch#

Three things. First, whether the “still goes further” claim holds in practice: if Astra’s real-world allowance now feels no better than its predecessor’s, the launch’s value story unravels. Second, December’s shared ChatGPT-Codex allowance, which will make the new counting visible in one place for the heaviest users. Third, whether the frustration turns into switching: OpenAI’s rivals have spent the autumn pitching themselves as the stable alternative, and a billing correction is exactly the kind of nudge that makes a trial account worth opening.

Frequently asked questions

Why am I hitting my GPT-6.2 limits faster?

Because OpenAI fixed a billing error that had discounted Astra and Sol usage since launch. The same work now counts fully against your allowance, so limits arrive 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than last week.

Will OpenAI charge me for the extra usage I got?

No. OpenAI says it will not charge for the difference on past usage; the fix only changes how future usage counts against your allowance.

What is the banked reset and how do I use it?

A one-time refresh of your allowance, added to every Go, Plus, Pro and Business account. Redeem it in Usage settings on the web or under Settings and Usage & billing in the desktop app — the mobile app does not offer it — once at least one covered allowance is below 80% remaining. On Plus it restores the five-hour and weekly allowances to 100%; on Go it restores the monthly allowance.

Does this change what my plan costs?

No. Subscription prices are unchanged; the correction affects how usage counts against existing limits, not what you pay. From December, Plus, Pro and Business move to a single token-based allowance shared across ChatGPT and Codex.
