Tec-Do launches Navos Pro, a 'Pro-Agent' that pitches itself on profit — GMV and ROI, not just answers

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · October 24, 2026 · 5 min read
TL;DR: Tec-Do launched Navos Pro at its Technology Growth Conference 2026 in Guangzhou, pitching it as the industry's first Pro-Agent for AI for Work. Built for cross-border ecommerce, international trade, marketing and business analysis, it combines a private brand knowledge base, round-the-clock monitoring, and creative and advertising execution tied to measurable outcomes such as GMV and ROI.
  • Who: Tec-Do (TecDo Technology Co., Limited), founded in 2017, which says it served more than 100,000 advertisers in 2025.
  • What: Navos Pro, launched at Technology Growth Conference 2026 (TGC 2026) in Guangzhou under the theme “AI is Profitable,” described by Tec-Do as the industry's first Pro-Agent.
  • When: announced October 24, 2026, in a GlobeNewswire release datelined Guangzhou, China.
  • Built for: cross-border ecommerce, international trade, marketing and business analysis — positioned by CEO Shuhao Li as a contender in “AI for Growth.”
  • Under the hood: specialized 32B, 27B and 5B parameter models, industry knowledge, more than 70 MCP integrations and more than 100 skills; Tec-Do says the platform monitors more than 1 billion data points daily.
  • The pitch, and its limits: outcomes are framed as GMV and ROI, but the release discloses no pricing or availability details, and “first Pro-Agent” is Tec-Do's own description, not an independently verified ranking.

Tec-Do launched Navos Pro at its Technology Growth Conference 2026 (TGC 2026) in Guangzhou, pitching the product as the industry's first Pro-Agent — an AI system that does not wait to be asked, but proactively finds work worth doing and ties it to business results. The conference theme, "AI is Profitable," is the whole argument in miniature: after years of AI sold on capability, Tec-Do wants to be judged on gross merchandise value (GMV) and return on investment (ROI).

The launch was framed by founder and CEO Shuhao Li, who mapped enterprise spending to three areas — marketing and sales, coding and product development, and management — and matched them to three directions he calls AI for Growth, AI for Innovation and AI for Administration. Navos Pro, he said, is a leading contender in AI for Growth. "We focus exclusively on results-driven AI for Work," Li said. AI pioneer Dr. Kevin Knight opened the conference with a keynote, "From Language to Action: The Next Leap in AI," identifying AI for Work as a major direction for AI applications.

Professional, proactive, profitable — what that means in practice

Navos Pro is designed for cross-border ecommerce, international trade, marketing and business analysis, and its pitch rests on three attributes. "Professional" means it builds a private knowledge base from a brand's website and retains business context, so outputs stay aligned with the brand's commercial objectives. "Proactive" means it monitors data around the clock to identify opportunities and recommend actions — while people retain decision-making authority. And "profitable" means it connects strategy, creative development and advertising execution to measurable outcomes, including GMV and ROI.

Underneath that pitch, Tec-Do cites specialized 32B, 27B and 5B parameter models, industry knowledge, more than 70 MCP integrations and more than 100 skills. The company says the platform monitors more than 1 billion data points daily. A Learning Loop connects results back to strategy optimization, while Navos RSI uses agentic reinforcement learning to improve its Agent Harness. The product portfolio pairs Navos Pro with Navos Lite, covering what Tec-Do describes as the end-to-end international marketing workflow.

From chatbots to Pro-Agents

Li's framing for the category is a shift in who initiates work: chatbots responded to requests, copilots assisted execution, and Pro-Agents proactively identify opportunities and deliver growth. That is a familiar arc in agent marketing this year, but Tec-Do's version is unusually explicit about the scoreboard — the product is not sold on how fluently it answers, but on whether the campaigns it touches sell more.

The company points to two partnerships as evidence it can already move that scoreboard. It says it became one of the first global technology partners for ChatGPT Ads in July 2026, helping brands raise ROI to 2.5 times previous levels. In August, it partnered with Tencent WorkBuddy to apply AI capabilities to international advertising. Both claims are Tec-Do's own; the release offers no independent verification, no customer names, and no baseline for that 2.5-times figure, so it should be read as a company claim, not a measured result.

OpenAI's presence at the event adds context rather than endorsement. Lorry Destainville, Monetization Partnerships at OpenAI, said ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion users weekly and that OpenAI is exploring Sponsored Agents and Visual Ads, thanking Tec-Do for collaborating on practical, measurable AI growth tools for brands. Three panels, moderated by Maeve Zhang, Tec-Do's Vice President of Branding and Public Relations, examined AI's impact on business, international expansion and ecosystem collaboration.

What is not in the announcement

For all its talk of measurable outcomes, the release discloses no pricing, no availability date beyond the launch itself, and no performance benchmarks for Navos Pro against competing agent platforms. It also does not say which of the 32B, 27B and 5B models does what, or how the "more than 70 MCP integrations" are distributed across channels. Those gaps matter for a product whose central claim is accountability: a system sold on ROI invites the question of what it costs and how its results are audited, and neither is answered here.

What the announcement does add is a second act. The conference concluded with Eileen Nie, Founding Partner and Head of Ecosystem Incubation and Strategic Investment at Tec-Do, announcing the Pro-Agent Global Challenge, inviting entrepreneurs worldwide to submit projects in agent architecture, AI marketing, AI content gateways and computing infrastructure, with access to capital, industry resources and global growth channels on offer. Li said AI for Growth is the first step, with plans to extend Tec-Do's capabilities into AI for Innovation and AI for Administration.

Tec-Do is not a household name in the West, but its scale is real enough to take seriously: founded in 2017, it describes itself as an AI marketing company powered by Tec-Chi multi-modal large language models and its Marketing Multi-Agent Platform, Navos, and says it served over 100,000 advertisers in 2025 across ecommerce, gaming, entertainment and local commerce. Navos Pro is its bid to turn that distribution into a new category claim. Whether "Pro-Agent" becomes a category or stays a slogan will depend on the numbers Tec-Do has not yet published — and on whether brands let an agent that initiates work keep initiating it once the first campaign underperforms.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Pro-Agent, in Tec-Do's framing?

A step beyond chatbots and copilots: where chatbots responded to requests and copilots assisted execution, a Pro-Agent proactively identifies opportunities and delivers growth — in Navos Pro's case, for cross-border ecommerce, international trade, marketing and business analysis.

What does Navos Pro actually do day to day?

It builds a private knowledge base from a brand's website and retains business context, monitors data around the clock to identify opportunities and recommend actions while people retain decision-making authority, and connects strategy, creative development and advertising execution to measurable outcomes such as GMV and ROI. Tec-Do cites specialized 32B, 27B and 5B parameter models, more than 70 MCP integrations, more than 100 skills, and monitoring of more than 1 billion data points daily.

Is Navos Pro proven to raise ROI?

Not by anything independently verified in this announcement. Tec-Do says its July 2026 ChatGPT Ads partnership helped brands raise ROI to 2.5 times previous levels, but that is the company's own claim, with no customer names, baseline or audit disclosed — and the release gives no pricing, availability details or benchmarks for Navos Pro itself.
