Coforge launches CXNova, an agentic platform that hands the whole customer journey to AI

Indian IT services firm Coforge launched CXNova on September 30 — an AI-native customer experience platform built on a multi-agent architecture, with content, context, and canvas agents orchestrated through a centralized control plane. The market noticed: shares traded about 4% higher in Mumbai.

MDMarcus DoyleSenior Technology Writer · September 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Coforge launched CXNova on September 30, an AI-native customer experience (CX) platform designed to help enterprises deliver personalization, engagement, and continuous optimization across customer touchpoints. Built on a multi-agent architecture, CXNova turns disconnected AI, content, and channel systems into what the company calls a unified experience execution layer. The announcement, filed with Indian stock exchanges, sent the stock about 4% higher — a signal that investors see agentic CX as the next front in enterprise software.

The pitch is aimed squarely at a known enterprise frustration. Companies have spent heavily on AI, digital experience platforms, and data ecosystems, yet most still struggle to operationalize AI across the customer journey. CXNova answers with an agentic orchestration layer that, in the company's words, turns strategic intent into scalable, autonomous customer journeys that learn, adapt, and deliver business value.

Three agent types, one control plane#

At the foundation sit domain-specific ontologies and enterprise knowledge graphs that connect customer, content, product, journey, and operational intelligence — giving every agent a shared understanding of business context. On top of that, three specialized agent classes work together to understand, decide, create, and adapt experiences in real time:

  • Content agents dynamically generate, adapt, and optimize content using enterprise knowledge, structured data, and domain-trained models — aiming for personalization at scale without breaking brand compliance.
  • Context agents interpret identity, intent, behavior, and real-time signals to drive next-best-action decisioning across the customer journey.
  • Canvas agents dynamically assemble and deliver experiences across digital and physical touchpoints: web, mobile, conversational, and emerging interaction channels.

Those are reinforced by industry-specific agents with embedded language, knowledge, experience patterns, and decision models, plus a centralized agent orchestration and experience operations (XOps) control plane that coordinates autonomous agents, enterprise systems, and human expertise. The human-in-the-loop line matters: enterprises remain skittish about fully autonomous customer-facing decisions, and the control plane is Coforge's answer to governance.

Why now#

"At Coforge, we believe the next phase of digital transformation is not about adding more AI features. It's about orchestrating experiences that can act in the moment," said Kapil Tyagi, Senior Vice President and Head of Experience at Coforge. "CXNova brings together five integrated capability areas to power an autonomous, experience-led enterprise with governed decision-making, continuous learning, and scalable experiences that become more intelligent with every interaction."

The platform is positioned as ready for enterprise-scale deployment across travel, airlines, banking and financial services, insurance, and healthcare, and designed to integrate with existing enterprise ecosystems rather than rip-and-replace them. That last point is the adoption argument: no CIO wants a fifth experience platform.

The launch-day reaction#

Investors responded quickly. The Hindu BusinessLine's market blog reported Coforge shares trading about 4% higher at ₹1,795.70 on the NSE following the CXNova launch. The launch was one of three Coforge announcements on September 30 — the company was also recognized as an ET Edge Best Tech Brand 2026 and outlined its agentic-travel strategy with the Aeronova.AI platform for modern airline retailing. Agentic AI, in other words, is becoming Coforge's whole product story.

What to watch#

  • Customer names. The platform's enterprise-scale claims will need named deployments in travel, banking, or healthcare — the industries Coforge is targeting.
  • Governance proof. The XOps control plane is the answer to the industry's biggest agentic-CX anxiety: autonomous agents making brand-damaging decisions. Enterprises will want to see how it audits and overrules agents in practice.
  • The competitive field. Genesys, Salesforce, and others are racing to define agentic CX; Coforge is betting that an India-built, knowledge-graph-first orchestration layer can carve out a distinct lane.
